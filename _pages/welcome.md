Hi 👋, I am George Petropoulos. My greek name is Giorgos or Yiorgos or Yorgos (greek: Γιώργος), a common abbreviation of my birth name [Georgios (greek: Γεώργιος)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgios). 

I am based in Athens, Greece and I am currently working as an engineering manager of the Data & AI team in [ChartMogul](https://chartmogul.com). I like taking data and AI products all the way, from the first idea to a proof of concept, then to implementation, and finally to something people actually use. Recently I have been driving ChartMogul's AI initiatives, including a publicly launched MCP server that opens up ChartMogul analytics to AI clients, along with other features in the product.

This is how I have worked for a long time. I tend to spot a need, get buy-in, build it, and ship it. I did this with ChartMogul's migration from Postgres to ClickHouse, with founding its Data Engineering team and analytics stack, and with similar projects in analytics, fintech, hiring and R&D. Having worked in lots of different domains, I consider myself a versatile person who will understand the business and figure out what it's needed to improve it. I am an executor and problem solver by nature, and I always aim to help and encourage my team and colleagues to learn and grow themselves.

I have worked and experimented with lots of data and AI tools, databases, programming languages, libraries and cloud services. I am currently working with ClickHouse, Snowflake, dbt and MCP, and I think about how I can improve ChartMogul's data and AI architecture.

I am documenting my experiences from life, data and AI engineering, while also maintaining my cv into the [Resume](/cv) page. This way I hope to improve my technical (and not only) writing.
